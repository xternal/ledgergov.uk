Variation to Road Investment Strategy 3 (RIS3): 2026 to 2031
The third Road Investment Strategy (RIS3) was published on 26 March 2026 and sets out the government’s priorities for the strategic road network in England for the period from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2031.
Following publication of RIS3, the Defence Investment Plan was published on 30 June 2026. To support the government’s increased investment in defence and national security, the Department for Transport was asked to make savings from its roads funding, and as part of this proposed a variation to RIS3.
Consultation on the RIS3 variation
The Department for Transport undertook consultation on the proposed variation between 23 July and 14 September 2026. National Highways was separately consulted on the Secretary of State’s proposals, and the Office of Rail and Road provided independent advice on the proposals.
Changes to RIS3 following the Defence Investment Plan
Having taken into account the consultation responses, input from National Highways and supporting evidence relating to a wide range of factors such as safety, housing, economic growth, value for money, environment and deliverability and investment considerations, the Secretary of State has decided to vary RIS3 to prioritise crucial investment to our national defence, now and for the future.
The variation will reduce capital funding by £428 million over the first 4 years of the RIS3 period, from 2026-27 to 2029-30. This includes a £315 million reduction in ‘enhancements’ funding associated with the cancellation of the A46 Newark Bypass and A38 Derby Junctions schemes, and a £113 million reduction in ‘other capital’ funding.
The variation is focused in scope. It does not constitute a wider reconsideration of RIS3. The rest of RIS3, including the objectives and performance specification, remain unchanged.
This RIS3 variation will be published shortly and will update the RIS3 published on 26 March 2026.
