Grants up to £45 million boost UK growth as Ministry of Defence funds national munitions production
UK-based companies can now apply for grants of up to £45 million to establish new factories producing munitions.
Investment will create more than 1,000 skilled jobs, part of “factories of the future” initiative.
Grants will form part of the Government’s £1.5 billion pledge to establish an ‘always on’ munitions production pipeline.
UK-based companies can now apply for government grants of up to £45 million to establish new factories producing the explosives, propellants and other energetic materials essential for the Armed Forces’ munitions.
The Ministry of Defence has opened the first capital grants window under its energetics programme, marking a major step in delivering the Strategic Defence Review’s commitment to rebuild sovereign production of critical defence materials and strengthen the resilience of the nation’s defence supply chain.
This investment will help rebuild the UK’s sovereign defence manufacturing base, support regional economic growth, create more than 1,000 skilled jobs through the Government’s wider “factories of the future” initiative, and strengthen opportunities for UK industry in international markets. The grants will form part of the Government’s £1.5 billion pledge to establish an ‘always on’ munitions production pipeline and support the creation of at least six new energetics and munitions factories across the UK
Increasing domestic production of energetic materials will reduce national reliance on overseas suppliers, improving the UK’s ability to replenish and scale-up munitions production, strengthening national resilience and boosting UK growth simultaneously. Energetic materials are fundamental to modern weapons systems, providing the propulsion, explosive and warhead effects required for military capability.
Minister for Defence Procurement Luke Pollard said:
The war in Ukraine has shown the importance of being able to produce, replenish and scale our munitions at pace.
Today’s announcement moves us another step closer to that goal. We are backing UK industry to rebuild that capability at home - strengthening our supply chains, creating more than 1,000 jobs and investment across the nation, while ensuring our Armed Forces have the munitions they need to fight and win.
National Armaments within MOD has launched the first of three competitive investment windows. Individual grants will be available for successful projects, subject to affordability, value-for-money assessments and due diligence.
The programme follows extensive engagement with industry and the completion of 24 feasibility studies examining potential factory sites across the UK. These studies have provided detailed insight into the opportunities for rebuilding domestic production capacity and the investment required to bring new facilities into operation.
The competition is open to organisations capable of delivering energetics manufacturing projects in the UK, regardless of whether they participated in previous feasibility or engineering design work. The first capital grants window has been opened by The Ministry of Defence under its energetics programme.
The funding marks a major step in delivering the Strategic Defence Review’s commitment to rebuild sovereign production of critical defence materials and strengthen the resilience of the nation’s defence supply chain.
Applicants will be required to demonstrate that proposals are militarily useful, technically feasible, commercially viable and deliverable, while meeting relevant environmental, regulatory and safety requirements. Projects must also show the ability to attract private investment and operate on a sustainable long-term basis.
This investment will help rebuild the UK’s sovereign defence manufacturing base, support regional economic growth, create more than 1,000 skilled jobs through the Government’s wider “factories of the future” initiative, and strengthen opportunities for UK industry in international markets.
The first application window is now open and will close on 8 January 2027. Full details of eligibility requirements, assessment criteria and how to apply are available here. Two further investment windows are expected in 2027.
