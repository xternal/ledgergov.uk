New Chair appointed of the Leasehold Advisory Service
Professor Nick Hopkins has today (2 November 2026) been announced as the new Chair of the Leasehold Advisory Service (LEASE), where he will strengthen the organisation’s role in championing the rights of leaseholders and park home owners.
LEASE is a government-funded body which provides free advice and information to leaseholders and park homeowners in England and Wales, including on commonhold and building safety issues.
As Chair, Professor Hopkins will provide strategic direction for LEASE as it continues to modernise its services and support leaseholders and park home owners in understanding their rights.
He brings a wealth of experience in land and property law, following a decade as the Law Commissioner for Property, Family and Trust Law in England and Wales, which included leading the Commission’s work on residential leasehold and commonhold reform, as well as his current position as Professor of Land Law at University College London.
Professor Hopkins has been a prominent advocate for reforming the leasehold and commonhold system, and through his work with the Law Commission, helped develop proposals intended to give homeowners greater control over their buildings.
Professor Hopkins takes over from Martin Boyd who has led LEASE since 2023 and provided strong leadership throughout a significant transformation and modernisation of the organisation’s services.
Minister for Housing and Planning, Matthew Pennycook MP said:
I warmly congratulate Professor Nick Hopkins on his appointment as LEASE’s Chair, and I very much look forward to working with him through this critical period as we both provide leaseholders with relief and progress the reforms necessary to end the feudal leasehold system for good.
I am extremely grateful to Martin Boyd for the service and leadership he has provided over the past three years. As a result of his considerable energy, expertise and commitment, LEASE has improved materially to the benefit of leaseholders and homeowners alike.
Professor Nick Hopkins said:
I am delighted to have been appointed Chair of LEASE at this significant period of reform of the sector. I look forward to working with LEASE’s Board and staff to support leasehold homeowners and owners of park homes in England and Wales and promote their rights. Alongside this vital work, I welcome the opportunity to help prepare current and future homeowners for the transition to commonhold as the default tenure for flat ownership so that they can fully benefit from the advantages that it will bring.

Professor Nick Hopkins is now in-post and the appointment is expected to run for three years.
The government remains committed to improving the experience of leaseholders and the Prime Minister has confirmed the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Bill will be introduced to Parliament before the end of this year.
