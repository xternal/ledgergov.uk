Remote electronic monitoring requirement: large pelagic trawlers fishing in English waters
Remote electronic monitoring (REM) will become mandatory for large pelagic trawlers.
It is expected to be a statutory requirement from November 2028, subject to parliamentary timings.
REM will apply to:
large pelagic trawlers of all nationalities fishing in English waters
English-registered large pelagic trawlers wherever they operate
What is REM
REM is technology fitted to fishing vessels to collect detailed information about fishing activity and catches. This includes data on:
when a vessel is fishing
where it is fishing
what it is catching
REM systems typically use cameras, gear sensors and global positioning system (GPS) units to record this information.
What REM will be used for
REM will fill data gaps by providing reliable evidence about fishing activity.
Our priorities are to use REM to collect data and evidence on:
where fishing happens
what fish are caught
unwanted catches (for example, fish, marine mammals, seabirds and sharks)
Defra will review and analyse this information and use it to inform fisheries management, science and compliance.
Check if you will need REM
If you are using trawl gears mainly to fish for small pelagic species, you may be required to fit REM.
REM requirements will apply to:
large pelagic trawlers of all nationalities fishing in English waters
English-registered large pelagic trawlers wherever they operate
What is a large pelagic trawler?
A large pelagic trawler is defined as a pelagic fishing boat, 18 metres or more in length, equipped with at least one of the following:
a chilled sea water system (CSW)
a refrigerated sea water system (RSW)
freezer storage capabilities
Small pelagic species include mackerel, herring, horse mackerel, anchovy, sardine, blue whiting, argentines, sprat, albacore, pilchards or boarfish.
If you need to fit an REM system
A full technical specification, guidance and information about the approval process will be available in 2027.
If you are the vessel owner, you must make sure your vessel is fitted with an REM system and approved by Defra to fish in English waters by the time the legislation comes into force.
