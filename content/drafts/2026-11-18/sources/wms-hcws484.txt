Today, I am pleased to announce a historic expansion of the West of England Combined Authority to include North Somerset, extending the benefits of devolution to over 220,000 more people in England.
Following a thorough assessment of the joint proposal submitted by WECA and North Somerset Council, the Secretary of State is satisfied that the statutory test to expand the West of England Combined Authority has been met. This momentous step will be the first of its kind for a Mayoral Combined Authority. It will formalise a near decade long relationship between the two areas, who have worked closely together to see the region and its residents thrive.
As part of the expansion, the West of England Combined Authority will receive an additional £15 million within this financial year, subject to a business case, to help unlock local growth opportunities across the expanded region. Alongside today’s announcement, the National Housing Bank has committed up to £250 million of investment to boost housing and regeneration in the West of England.
Established in 2017, the West of England Combined Authority has a proven track record of delivering for residents across its footprint. The region has seen huge strides taken to improve transport networks with new electric buses and depots, designated walking and cycling routes as well new stations being built at Portishead and Pill which will see over 50,000 people reconnected to the railway. The West of England Combined Authority has provided Adult Skills services and apprenticeship programmes for over 11,000 people each year. The Authority has also helped unlock thousands of new homes and jobs for local residents through the nationally significant development of Bristol Temple Quarter, and Brabazon and the West Innovation Arc.
This Government is committed to widening and deepening devolution across the country, delivering growth in all regions. The expansion of the West of England Combined Authority will extend to North Somerset the benefits of mayoral devolution, while enabling the West of England Combined Authority to operate at greater strategic scale across the whole region. All of this will better empower the region to benefit its communities, boost economic growth and drive change.
Next Steps
Government is committed to empowering places through devolution, with the aim of achieving full devolution in England by the end of 2028. Today’s announcement will take us one step closer to this.
We will now begin the formal legislative process to provide for the expansion with the intention to conclude by early 2027.
