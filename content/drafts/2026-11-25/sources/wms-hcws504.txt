The consultation paper ‘Licensing for the Sale of Knives’ was published on 16 December 2025. It invited comments on Government proposals to introduce a licensing system for sellers and importers of knives and bladed articles. The consultation ran for a 10-weeks period, closing on Tuesday 24 February 2026.
In the consultation, we proposed introducing registration through a licensing scheme for sales of knives and bladed articles. We sought views on whether businesses which sell knives and bladed articles should be required to hold a licence and whether this should also apply to private sellers.
Alongside the licensing for sellers of knives, we also proposed introducing licences for the importation of knives and bladed articles. It is recognised that stronger regulation of the UK market could encourage people to look to buy from sellers based abroad and therefore import licensing will be important in preventing circumvention of sellers’ licensing.
A total of 3551 responses to the consultation paper were received. These have now been analysed and considered, and the Government Response will be published today setting out our next steps:
We will introduce a licensing scheme for the sale of knives that will apply to both businesses and private sellers;
We will introduce a licensing scheme for importation whereby sellers based abroad will need to appoint an intermediary company that will ensure that the sale is compliant with domestic legislation. The intermediary company will be responsible for ensuring that the articles are delivered in compliance with UK law.
We will look at the feedback received from responses to the consultation in relation to specific exemptions in relation to heritage collections, crafts and trades and whether there should be exemptions in these areas.
We will also engage with stakeholders such as small businesses to ensure that the scheme imposes reasonable demands that are achievable, balancing business impact with public safety concerns.
A copy of the consultation response will be placed in the Libraries of both Houses of Parliament and published on Gov.UK.
