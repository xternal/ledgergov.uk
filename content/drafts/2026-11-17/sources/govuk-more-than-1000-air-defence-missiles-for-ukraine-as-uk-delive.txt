More than 1,000 air defence missiles for Ukraine as UK delivers £240 million in winter support
£240 million in air defence support will help Ukraine defend itself this winter against Putin’s barbaric attacks.
Support includes more than 1,000 additional short-range air defence missiles, and more than a dozen air defence radars.
More than 50 countries expected to come together to pledge support for Ukraine as part of Ukraine Defence Contact Group, providing military kit for Ukraine co-chaired by the Defence Secretary at NATO HQ in Brussels today.
More than 1,000 new air defence systems proven against Russia’s jet-powered drones will be supplied by the UK to strengthen Ukraine’s defences this winter.
The Defence Secretary has today (17 November 2026) announced the new support as he co-chaired the 50-nation strong Ukraine Defence Contact Group at NATO Headquarters in Brussels.
The £240 million air defence package for Ukraine forms part of the £500 million announced at the Budget to plug urgent gaps in Ukraine’s air defences this winter.
The package includes more than 1,000 short-range air defence missiles and launchers. These systems have proven effective against the Iranian-designed jet-powered Shahed drones that Russia is using to strike Ukrainian cities and infrastructure.
Deliveries to Ukraine’s armed forces will begin from mid-December, building on the 400 surface-to-air missiles the UK has already supplied Ukraine ahead of winter. Under this package the UK will also supply over a dozen air defence radars to help Ukraine identify incoming strikes, providing critical support to its defence through the winter.
This support comes in response to direct requests from Ukraine’s leaders, and forms part of the £500 million package for Ukraine this winter announced at the Budget, on top of the wider £10 billion commitment to Ukraine. The Prime Minister will outline the remaining support at the Coalition of the Willing meeting on Thursday.
Defence Secretary Wes Streeting MP said:
In Ukraine, President Putin uses winter as a weapon. That’s why we must continue to convert our steadfast commitment for Ukraine into capability that will help defend against barbaric Russian attacks.
We are using funding the Chancellor pledged at the Budget to support Ukraine this winter to deliver over 1,000 air defence missiles, as part of our ongoing commitment to mobilise allies and strengthen Ukraine’s defences.
Ukraine’s frontline is the frontline for freedom and democracy, and we will stand with them for as long as it takes.
The support includes missiles from Thales Belfast and air defence radars sourced from the UK and Europe.
Today’s support pledge comes as the UK, alongside France, is currently leading the Multinational Force Ukraine’s first major exercise in Poland this month. Exercise Koalicja tests how to move, protect and supply a force that would help rebuild Ukraine’s Armed Forces once the fighting ends.
Defence Secretary Wes Streeting and German Defence Minister Boris Pistorius today co-chair the Ukraine Defence Contact Group, which brings together almost 50 nations to coordinate and deliver vital military support for Ukraine. This is the group’s 37th meeting and takes place at NATO HQ in Brussels.
Today also sees the UK, Sweden and Ukraine sign a Letter of Intent committing to the principle of providing Swedish Gripen fighter jets to Ukraine with a formidable range of battle-proven weapons systems, ensuring Ukraine has the capabilities it needs when it needs them.
This follows this year’s announcement of €300 million in investment to help deliver Gripens to Ukraine. Companies across the UK supply critical components for the Gripen, including the radar and landing gear, with more than 30% of each aircraft manufactured in the UK.
