Alan Milburn has today published the final report of his Review into Young People and Work. I want to thank Alan and his review team for their outstanding work over the last year, and for the landmark report they have published. The Government strongly endorses the need for a new settlement for young people, as set out in the Report. It will consider the report in full alongside the recommendations of the Timms Review which are expected shortly.
I commissioned this Report in November 2025 to understand the root causes of a national youth participation crisis that has been building for many years and has left nearly one million young people outside of education, employment or training. The Report sets out the scale and causes of that crisis, and makes recommendations for how Government and beyond should respond.
This Government has already begun that work. We have committed an additional £2.5 billion investment in the Youth Guarantee, supporting nearly a million young people with up to 500,000 opportunities to earn or learn. That includes the Jobs Guarantee which provides a fully-funded six-month job for young people who are long-term unemployed. The report recommends going further on our Jobs Guarantee, and I am pleased to announce today that, from spring next year, we will make this support available sooner, from 12 months of unemployment rather than 18 months. The Government welcomes the recognition of this action in the Report and agrees with the conclusion that we must go further.
However, the Report makes clear that incremental changes to the current system cannot meet the scale of the challenges we face. Instead, we must deliver a wholly new settlement for young people. A new system capable of changing young people’s lives for the better, that protects those who will never be able to work, provides additional support where it is needed, and gives every other young person a route into work, education or training.
Underpinning this system needs to be a commitment to provide young people with real opportunities and support. That is why, today, we are committing to provide an Opportunity and Support Guarantee, as Milburn has recommended, so that every young person who is at risk of becoming NEET has a credible route into education, training, an apprenticeship, high-quality work experience, or a job.
The Report recommends delivering this Guarantee through a new locally-led service, because local leaders know their places and communities. We are therefore committing to a service that brings together a single, coherent offer across employment, skills, health and wider support at the local level, delivered by local and regional leaders.
To drive changes forward, I am also announcing the creation of the Youth Opportunities Unit, based in the Office of the Prime Minister and Cabinet. The Unit will be responsible for driving cross-government action on youth opportunity. The Prime Minister will appoint a Chair to lead this Unit’s work across government.
As the Chancellor set out at the Autumn Budget, the Government will consider the Report’s recommendations alongside those of the Timms Review of Personal Independence Payment, which will be published shortly. We will set out detailed proposals in a White Paper in early 2027 for how we will deliver a new settlement that puts opportunity at the heart of the system.
